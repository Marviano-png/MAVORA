What makes MAVORA different

Trust & Safety

MAVORA is designed around verification, clear records, secure communication, reporting, disputes and payment states. Never share your password, OTP or payment PIN with another user.

About MAVORA

MAVORA is a marketplace built to connect people who need work done with people who can do it, across online and local opportunities.

Our mission

Turn skills into opportunity, experience and income.

What we believe

  • Clear job information builds trust.
  • People should have a fair way to discover opportunities.
  • Payments and work status should be understandable.
  • Safety and privacy are part of the product, not an afterthought.

Terms of Service

Use MAVORA lawfully, give accurate information, and honour agreed work and payment obligations. You are responsible for your account and everything done through it. MAVORA may suspend accounts that break these terms.

Privacy Policy

What we collect: email, name, username, phone number, profile and portfolio content, messages, job, order and payment records, and, if you verify your identity, your ID document and ID number.

Why: to run your account, prevent fraud, process payments and withdrawals, and resolve disputes.

Who can see your ID: only authorised MAVORA staff who review verification requests. Documents are kept in private storage and are never shown publicly.

Service providers: Supabase (hosting and storage), Paystack (payments), Termii (SMS codes) and EmailJS (email).

How long we keep it: [retention period] after you close your account, except records the law requires us to keep.

Your rights: under the Nigeria Data Protection Act you can ask to access, correct or delete your data. Use Settings, then Request account deletion, or write to [Contact email]. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Refunds and Cancellation

Refunds follow the recorded state of the job or order. If work is not delivered, differs materially from what was agreed, or an item is faulty or not as described, you can cancel or open a dispute, and where the claim is valid you are entitled to a refund or replacement. MAVORA does not accept "no refund" terms.

Disputes

Prohibited Items and Services

Payments and Safety

Keep payments inside MAVORA's approved payment flow. Never share your password, OTP or card details, and never pay or accept payment outside MAVORA for work found here.
